Analysis & Diagnosis

Instructions

  1. Click "Setup Logging" to inject logging into the page
  2. Navigate to and load a pruefungssimulation exam
  3. Fill in some answers (e.g., 2a, 2b, 3a, 3b)
  4. Submit the exam ("Auswertung durchführen")
  5. Check the "Flow Trace Log" to see what happened at each step
  6. Click "Analyze Collected Data" to see the diagnosis

The log will show:

  • When exam is loaded and what data is received
  • When answers are collected and their values
  • When backend response is received
  • When results are being matched and mapped
  • Any mismatches or problems in the flow
